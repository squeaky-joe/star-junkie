# The Star Junkies Manifesto

## Why we're here
The verse is huge, beautiful, and a little ridiculous, and it's better with friends. Star Junkies exists for people who want to log in, hang out, and enjoy the game without a schedule, a rank ladder, or a to-do list hanging over them.

## What we believe

1. **Fun comes first.** If it isn't fun, we don't do it. Whether that's hauling cargo, exploring a moon nobody's heard of, or just sitting in a hangar talking nonsense, the goal is a good time.

2. **No pressure, ever.** There are no mandatory events, no activity quotas, and no guilt for disappearing for a week. Life happens. Jump in when you can, and we'll be glad to see you.

3. **Chill is the standard.** We're a relaxed crew. Disagreements get settled with a laugh, not a lecture. Be kind, be easygoing, and don't take the small stuff seriously.

4. **Everyone's welcome.** Veteran pilots and day-one rookies are equals here. Nobody gets mocked for crashing, getting lost, or blowing up the ship they just bought. We've all done it, and it usually makes the best story.

5. **Play your way.** Trader, pirate, explorer, miner, or someone who just likes flying around, it's all fine. We don't have a "correct" way to play. The game is a sandbox, so we use it like one.

6. **Let the verse decide.** We don't plan everything. Some of the best nights start with "what do you want to do?" and end somewhere nobody expected. We say yes to the random stuff and see what comes about.

7. **Look out for each other.** If someone needs a hand with a mission, a ride, or a rescue, we show up. Teamwork here means being a good crew, not a tightly run machine.

## What we aren't
We're not a hardcore military unit, a min-max trading empire, or a grind-for-glory guild. If that's your thing, there are great orgs for it. We're the place you come to unwind.

## Our promise
Star Junkies will stay laid back, welcoming, and low-stress. We'll keep the vibes good, the pressure off, and the door open.

## Join us
Founded in 2956, we're a brand-new crew with plenty of room and plenty of stars left to see. Come hang out, chill, and fly with us. Whatever comes about, we'll figure it out together.
