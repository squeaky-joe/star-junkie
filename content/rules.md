# Star Junkies Rules

1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, or bullying.
2. **Keep it chill.** Leave drama at the door.
3. **No griefing members.** Playful chaos is fine only when everyone's in on it.
4. **No spam or self-promo** without asking first.
5. **Keep content appropriate** and use the right channels.
6. **Listen to the Co-Pilots.** If you have an issue, message one of them.
7. **Have fun!** That's the whole point.
