**Star Junkies** was founded in the year **2956** (that's **2026-11-18** out here in the real world).

We started with a simple idea: a Star Citizen org that's about hanging out, chilling, and playing together. No quotas, no pressure, no mandatory fleet nights. Just good company in the verse.

Since then we've been a crew of friends who fly, haul, mine, explore and sometimes just sit in the hangar and talk. If that sounds like your kind of org, pull up a seat.
